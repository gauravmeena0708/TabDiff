Adult TabDiff Method Significance Interpretation

Experiment output: experiments/adult_tabdiff_method_significance

Compared methods: none, stochastic, midpoint. Significance rule: global Friedman p < 0.05, with paired Wilcoxon/Holm checks for pairwise differences.

Main inference The methods are not behaviorally identical.
Best overall pattern stochastic most often improves fidelity and distribution similarity.
No clear utility gain mle, tstr, and c2st were not globally significant.
No broad privacy change Most csdeval privacy metrics were not globally significant.

Interpretation

The variation mainly changes fidelity and distribution-shape metrics. It does not show strong evidence of changing downstream utility, constraint satisfaction, or most privacy metrics in this run.

stochastic is the only method that consistently separates from the other two. It is better on TabDiff density metrics and several csdeval fidelity metrics, especially wasserstein, conditional_distribution_fidelity, marginal_distortion, and entropy_ratio.

midpoint does not show a clear advantage. It is usually close to none, and often significantly worse than stochastic.

Practical conclusion: use stochastic if the goal is better conditional distribution fidelity. Do not claim utility or privacy improvement from this run alone.

What The Better Fidelity Metrics Mean

csdeval/wasserstein

This measures how close the synthetic and real value distributions are. A better score means generated samples are closer to the real data in terms of feature values. In this run, stochastic was best: 0.959184 versus 0.934912 for none and 0.928617 for midpoint.

csdeval/conditional_distribution_fidelity

This checks whether the distribution inside the requested condition matches the real conditional slice. For example, among rows where education=11th, the remaining fields should look like real education=11th rows. stochastic was best: 0.982840.

csdeval/marginal_distortion

This measures distortion in individual column distributions. A better score means columns such as age, workclass, occupation, income, and hours-per-week are less distorted relative to real data. The stochastic gain is statistically stable but small in absolute size.

csdeval/entropy_ratio

This reflects diversity or spread. A better entropy ratio means the synthetic data is less collapsed and has diversity closer to the real data. stochastic again had the best average value.
